YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy Policy

This page explains the information Swiper may process when you use the game and its online features.

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Information you provide

Depending on the features you use, Swiper may process information you submit, such as an account email address, username, profile details, messages, feedback, reports, and community levels you create or publish. Do not include sensitive personal information in usernames, messages, reports, or level content.

Gameplay and technical information

When online features are used, the service may process gameplay records such as scores, game mode, level information, account identifiers, report records, and timestamps. The game may also use browser storage or similar technologies to remember local preferences and guest progress. The information available depends on the feature and your settings.

Service providers

Swiper uses Supabase services for certain online account, database, and game features. Information needed for those features is sent to the service for processing and storage. Those services operate under their own terms and privacy practices. Some profile details, such as a player's username or display name, may be visible to other players through public game features. Private account information is not intended to be displayed in the public player directory.

Cookies, advertising, and third parties

Swiper may load third-party resources needed for features such as web fonts or its online services. Swiper does not currently intentionally display Google AdSense ads. If Google AdSense or another advertising service is enabled in the future, that provider and its partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or similar identifiers to serve, measure, and personalize ads as permitted by law and your choices. This policy must be updated to describe the actual advertising technology and choices before advertising is enabled. You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

For information about Google's data practices on partner sites, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

How information is used

Information may be used to operate gameplay and accounts, save scores, publish community levels, provide social features, respond to support requests, investigate reports, protect the service, and maintain security. Information may be retained for as long as needed for these purposes or as required by applicable law.

Your choices and requests

You can choose not to use account-based features. You may contact Swiper to ask about access, correction, or deletion requests. Some records may need to be retained for security, moderation, or legal reasons. We may need to verify a request before acting on it.

Children's privacy

Swiper does not ask players to post personal information publicly. Children should use the service with the guidance of a parent or guardian. Parents or guardians who have a privacy question or request can contact us using the details below.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the game or its service providers change. The latest version will be published on this page. Questions or privacy requests can be sent through the Contact page.

Swiper is a browser-based arcade game. Play fairly and treat other players with respect.
