Community Guidelines

Help keep Swiper fair, welcoming, and fun for everyone.

Play Swiper

Be respectful

  • Do not harass, threaten, bully, or target other players.
  • Do not use hateful, discriminatory, or sexually explicit language.
  • Do not impersonate another player, moderator, or the Swiper team.
  • Do not share another person's private or identifying information.

Publish appropriate levels

  • Only publish levels you have the right to share.
  • Do not use level titles, descriptions, or designs to threaten, harass, or advertise prohibited material.
  • Do not upload content that infringes someone else's copyright or other rights.
  • Do not attempt to exploit the game, accounts, or other players.

Use reporting responsibly

Swiper has an existing reporting system for players, community levels, and scores. Use the report controls when you encounter content that appears to break these guidelines. Include accurate details that help explain the concern.

Do not submit reports to retaliate, spam the system, or make claims you know are false. A report is a request for review, not proof that a violation occurred.

Moderation

Content and accounts may be reviewed when a report is received or when a potential violation is otherwise identified. Swiper may restrict access to content or features, remove content, or take account action when appropriate. Review timing and outcomes are not guaranteed, and not every report will result in a change.

For an account appeal, use the in-game appeal/contact link or the Contact page.

Swiper is a browser-based arcade game. Play fairly and treat other players with respect.
