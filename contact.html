Reporting a problem

For a player, community level, or score that appears to violate the rules, use the report feature in the game when available. Include a clear and accurate reason. For account restrictions or appeals, contact support and include your username and a brief explanation. Never send your password.

Privacy requests

For privacy questions or requests about personal information, use the support email above and put “Privacy Request” in the subject. Do not include passwords or other sensitive information.

Swiper is a browser-based arcade game. Play fairly and treat other players with respect.
