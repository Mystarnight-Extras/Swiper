THE GAME

About Swiper

Swiper is a browser-based arcade game built around one simple challenge: make the right move at the right moment.

Play Swiper

A simple idea with room to master

During a run, your player moves between lanes to avoid incoming spikes. Every decision is about timing, attention, and learning the rhythm of the obstacles. Runs are quick to start, but improving a score takes practice.

Swiper is designed to work in a modern mobile or desktop browser. On touch screens, swipe to change lanes. On supported devices, keyboard and on-screen movement controls are also available.

Choose how to play

Practice gives you space to learn the movement. Bot Play lets you test your timing against a computer-controlled opponent. Quick Play is designed for online matchmaking when players are available.

Make something

Community Levels lets players build and publish obstacle patterns, then browse and play levels made by others. Published creations are subject to the community rules and reporting tools.

Compete and connect

Swiper includes score and leaderboard features, player profiles, and social features. Some online features require an account and an internet connection.

Built for fair play

Players can report players, community levels, and scores they believe violate the rules. Reports help identify content that may need review. Do not submit false, abusive, or repeated reports.

Swiper is an evolving project. Features, availability, and game balance may change as the game is improved.

Swiper is a browser-based arcade game. Play fairly and treat other players with respect.
